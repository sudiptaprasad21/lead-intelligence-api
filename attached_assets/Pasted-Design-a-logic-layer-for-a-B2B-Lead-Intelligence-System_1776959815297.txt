Design a logic layer for a B2B Lead Intelligence System that maps lead scores to segments and triggers corresponding actions.

---

## 1. Segmentation Logic (Score → Segment → Action)

* If score >= 80:

  * segment = "Hot"
  * actions = ["Immediate Sales Call", "WhatsApp Outreach"]

* If score >= 60 and < 80:

  * segment = "Warm"
  * actions = ["Email Outreach", "SDR Follow-up"]

* If score >= 40 and < 60:

  * segment = "Nurture"
  * actions = ["Automated Email Workflow", "Retargeting Trigger"]

* If score < 40:

  * segment = "Cold"
  * actions = ["Low Priority Drip Campaign", "Periodic Re-evaluation"]

---

## 2. Action Logic: Hot Leads (Path 1)

### A. Immediate Sales Call Task Creation

* Create task in CRM/task system in real-time
* Priority: High

#### Working Hours:

* Monday–Saturday, 09:00 AM – 08:00 PM

#### Rules:

* Within working hours → Assign immediately
* Outside working hours → Schedule next working day at 10:00 AM

#### Assignment:

* Round-robin OR geography-based routing

#### Task Payload:

* Lead details (name, company, role)
* Score + key intent signals
* Source + timestamp

---

### B. WhatsApp Outreach

* Trigger immediately after qualification

#### AI Inputs:

* Role, company, intent signals, segment

#### Constraints:

* 2–4 lines
* Personalized + action-driven

#### Fallback:

* Predefined template

---

## 3. Action Logic: Warm Leads

### A. Email Outreach

* Trigger within 5–15 minutes of lead qualification

#### Type:

* AI-personalized OR semi-templated

#### Inputs:

* Lead role, company, industry, intent signals

#### Content Goal:

* Introduce value proposition
* Encourage demo booking

#### Constraints:

* Clear CTA (demo link)
* Professional tone (B2B)

#### Fallback:

* Static email template if AI fails

---

### B. SDR Follow-Up Task

* Create task for SDR team

#### SLA:

* Action within 24 hours

#### Priority:

* Medium

#### Scheduling Rules:

* If within working hours → assign immediately
* If outside working hours → next working day at 11:00 AM

#### Task Includes:

* Lead summary
* Email already sent (context)
* Suggested talking points (optional AI-generated)

---

### C. Engagement Tracking

* Track:

  * Email opens
  * Link clicks
  * Demo bookings

#### Trigger:

* If engagement detected → re-score lead
* If score crosses 80 → move to "Hot" flow

---

## 4. Action Logic: Nurture Leads

### A. Automated Email Workflow

* Add lead to multi-step drip campaign

#### Structure:

* Day 0 → Intro email
* Day 3 → Value/educational content
* Day 7 → Case study / proof
* Day 14 → Soft CTA (demo invite)

#### Personalization:

* Light personalization (industry/use case)

---

### B. Retargeting Trigger

* Add lead to retargeting audience (ads)

#### Channels:

* LinkedIn / Google (simulated if needed)

#### Objective:

* Reinforce awareness + drive return visits

---

### C. Engagement Monitoring

* Track:

  * Email interaction
  * Website revisit
  * Content engagement

#### Logic:

* If engagement increases:
  → Recalculate score
  → Upgrade to Warm or Hot

---

### D. Re-scoring Frequency

* Automatically re-evaluate every 3–7 days
* OR on any new activity event

---

## 5. Action Logic: Cold Leads

### A. Low Priority Drip Campaign

* Add to long-term, low-frequency campaign

#### Frequency:

* 1 email every 2–3 weeks

#### Content:

* Thought leadership
* Industry insights
* No aggressive CTA

---

### B. Periodic Re-evaluation

* Re-score every 14 days

#### Trigger Conditions:

* New activity
* Data enrichment updates

---

### C. Suppression Rules

* If no engagement after X cycles (e.g., 60–90 days):
  → Mark as inactive OR archive

---

### D. Minimal Resource Allocation

* No SDR or sales intervention
* Fully automated handling

---

## 6. System Requirements

* All actions must be event-driven (triggered on score updates)

* Ensure idempotency (no duplicate actions)

* Maintain action logs:

  * action_type
  * timestamp
  * status (success/failure)

* Store:

  * segment
  * action history
  * engagement signals

---

## 7. Output Format (API / UI)

Return:

{
"score": number,
"segment": "Hot | Warm | Nurture | Cold",
"actions_triggered": [string],
"next_action": string,
"scheduled_time": datetime (if applicable),
"status": "success | pending"
}
